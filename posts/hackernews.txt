TITLE: Show HN: Git-verified open-source investigation — 1,822 pages, 10,815 nodes, 16M edges

PRIMAL: architecture

I built an open-source public records investigation as a pair of Zola static sites served from a 2GB VPS.

The investigation documents a charter school network in Detroit — a superintendent with 9 criminal convictions running $4.9M/year in publicly-funded schools, connected to judges and political figures.

The technical infrastructure:
- 2 Zola static sites: 1,822 pages total (1,199 tuebor + 623 detroit)
- Git-verified: every page committed, timestamped, BLAKE3-hashed
- Network graph: 10,815 nodes, 16M edges
- 31 services on a membrane architecture (WireGuard mesh, Caddy, custom Python pipeline)
- Structured data: JSON-LD on every page, llms.txt, sitemap, content manifest
- 100% open: robots.txt allows all crawlers, AI agents, archives
- License: CC-BY-SA-4.0 (content), AGPL-3.0 (code)

Sites: detroit.primals.eco (evidence) and tuebor.primals.eco (analysis)
Repo: github.com/defendDetroit/publicRecord

What I'd like feedback on: the entity-relationship methodology and whether this kind of structured public records work has legs as a reproducible framework.
